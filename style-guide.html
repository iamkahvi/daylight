daylight style guide

These examples use the app styles. Use the sample IDs to refer to each style. Return to the app.

countdowns

Hours, minutes, and seconds use two digits each.

C1 · countdown

sunrise is in 02:05:09

times

Times use a 12-hour clock, two-digit hours, and uppercase AM or PM.

T1 · arc time
05:50PM
T2 · chart time

durations

Durations use hours and minutes. The chart also shows a difference for a selected date.

D1 · arc duration
10h34m
D2 · chart duration

time axis labels

The year chart does not show time axis labels. This example shows the previous style.

A1 · time axis (previous)

date axis labels
